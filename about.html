Kiko Global Trade

Kiko Global Trade operates as the international arm of Util Kiko, a Brazilian company with over five decades of market presence in manufacturing and distribution.

Our role is to structure cross-border trade operations between South America and the United States, supporting commercial alignment, documentation coordination, and logistics planning across multiple product categories.

About Util Kiko

Founded more than 50 years ago, Util Kiko is recognized in Brazil for its large-scale production and distribution of disposable food packaging and related consumer goods. The company serves major retail chains and operates with an extensive national distribution network.

The industrial foundation of Util Kiko supports the international expansion initiatives conducted through Kiko Global Trade.
