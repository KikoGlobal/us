About Util Kiko

Founded more than five decades ago, Util Kiko serves food retail chains and supermarket networks throughout Brazil. Its portfolio includes disposable food packaging, household goods, and related consumer products distributed nationally.

The company maintains an extensive product catalog and established logistics structure supporting large-scale distribution.
